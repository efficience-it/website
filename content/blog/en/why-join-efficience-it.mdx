## 1. At Efficience IT, people come first

Most IT companies put the human side after technology, even though it is essential to employee well-being. It is the same at Efficience IT. That means team-building events, happy hours, darts, table football, and the list goes on.

### A setting built for well-being

At Efficience IT, well-being is more than a sentence on a careers page. It shows up in concrete actions every day. Regular events, whether friendly evenings, team lunches or sports activities, create a real sense of belonging. Every new team member gets a personalized onboarding path to feel at ease from day one. The goal is simple: everyone comes to work with a smile, in an environment where kindness is not an empty word but something everyone experiences.

## 2. Daily challenges

Do you want to join a dynamic environment and thrive in it?

Efficience IT team members regularly work on assignments with real responsibilities and real challenges. Our team of enthusiasts, 100% focused on [Symfony](/article/pourquoi-choisir-symfony-pour-vos-projets) (in French), a modern framework, goes the extra mile to fit our clients' projects. Based in the Hauts-de-France region, our [Symfony agency in Lille](/agence-symfony-lille) (in French) works on ambitious projects across the region and beyond.

### Varied and stimulating projects

Working at Efficience IT means never falling into a routine. Our projects span very different sectors: healthcare, logistics, finance, media and education. Each assignment brings its own new technical problems, whether designing a high-performance architecture, optimizing response times or building complex data flows. This variety gives developers rare versatility and strengthens their technical expertise on real cases, with a measurable impact on the final product delivered to the client.

## 3. Your voice is heard

Do you want to make an impact in the company? Do you want your opinion to count? At Efficience IT, the whole team is consulted on how the company is organized and run. The idea is to test, measure and iterate. Our collaborative management is open to everyone's suggestions to value the diversity of viewpoints.

### Flat, participatory management

In practice, this means regular retrospectives where every team member can suggest improvements to processes, tools or the way work is organized. Important decisions are made collectively, and proposals are judged on their relevance, not on the rank of the person making them. An intern has as much right to propose a change as a senior developer. This culture of constant feedback lets the company evolve quickly and adapt to its team members' expectations, while giving each person responsibility for building the collective.

## 4. You shape your own future

Do you want to take your future into your own hands? At Efficience IT, upskilling is guaranteed thanks to the close presence of other developers working on the same framework as you. Technology watch, meet-ups and conferences also help developers stay up to date on technologies and industry practices.

Even apprentices and interns are assigned to assignments with varied responsibilities, and they get real guidance from management and more experienced colleagues every day.

### A tailored progression plan

Every developer receives individual follow-up to define their career goals and the steps to reach them. Whether you want to specialize in a specific technical area, move toward a tech lead role or explore new horizons such as software architecture, Efficience IT supports you along the way. We encourage attending [conferences like Forum PHP](/article/retour-sur-le-forum-php-2024) (in French) or SymfonyLive, and internal knowledge sharing is part of our culture. To structure this progression, we offer [the 6 steps to upskill on Symfony](/article/les-6-etapes-pour-monter-en-competences-sur-symfony) (in French) as a guiding thread. Code reviews, pair programming and knowledge-sharing sessions are all opportunities to learn from your peers.

## 5. Be at the heart of development

Our first goal at Efficience IT is to bring added value to our clients through digital technology, and in particular through Symfony. We build expertise and deliver quality end-to-end projects that have a real impact on how our clients operate.

### From design to going to production

At Efficience IT, developers are not confined to writing code against frozen specifications. They take an active part in every phase of the project: business needs analysis, technical design, development, testing, deployment and maintenance. This global view helps them understand the context the code lives in and propose solutions that truly fit. Using Symfony as the common thread guarantees strong technical consistency, while leaving room for innovation on complementary tools: API Platform, [Docker](/article/pourquoi-docker-est-indispensable-en-production-aujourdhui) (in French), robust CI/CD pipelines and advanced caching strategies. Every line of code has a purpose, and every delivered feature answers a concrete need.

## 6. Live your values in a committed company

Do you want to be part of a company committed to environmental values? Efficience IT is a start-up that aims to be ethical and responsible. We are always looking for new Green IT practices and do our best to adopt them, notably by limiting our carbon footprint.

Finally, diversity of profiles and inclusiveness are among our greatest strengths, and you will see it when you join us.

### Concrete commitments for responsible technology

Efficience IT's commitment to Green IT goes beyond good intentions. The team strives to apply eco-design practices in its development work: optimizing queries to reduce server load, choosing hosting providers that care about their energy impact, and raising clients' awareness of digital sobriety. The learning culture also extends to IT security, as shown by [the Chocoblast, our playful approach to awareness through games](/article/le-chocoblast-un-premier-pas-vers-la-securite-par-le-jeu) (in French). On the human side, inclusiveness is lived every day through an open hiring policy that values unconventional backgrounds and complementary profiles. Joining Efficience IT means joining a team that believes technology can drive progress without compromising the planet's future.

## 7. Work in flexible, innovative offices

Personal fulfillment and a modern, comfortable work environment: we practice Flex Office. You are free to work anywhere in the offices, and even remotely. Everything is designed to foster team spirit and exchanges between colleagues.

### A work environment that fits everyone

Flex Office at Efficience IT means choosing the space that best matches what you are doing right now: a quiet corner to focus on a complex technical problem, an open space to collaborate in pairs, or a dedicated room for video calls with clients. Remote work is fully built into the organization, with the tools needed to keep communication smooth from a distance. This flexibility rests on the trust we give each team member and on the belief that a suitable work environment is a key factor in productivity and fulfillment.

**Do you like being challenged? Do you want to shake things up? Welcome aboard!**
