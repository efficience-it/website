## Symfony Certifications: Twig, Symfony, and Sylius

Are you a developer looking to strengthen your technical profile? Official certifications validate expertise, boost professional credibility, and open career advancement opportunities for backend, frontend, and fullstack developers. In an increasingly competitive job market, holding a recognized certification helps you stand out to recruiters, clients, and peers. It shows a personal investment in mastering your craft and is a mark of seriousness for companies looking for qualified profiles.

## Symfony, Twig, and Sylius Certifications

### Symfony Certified Developer

The official Symfony certification is a key credential for PHP developers. The exam covers 14 essential topics, including HTTP fundamentals, Symfony architecture, routing, templating with Twig, form handling, validation, dependency injection, security, HTTP caching, console commands, and automated testing.

The exam takes place online through SymfonyConnect, lasts 90 minutes, and contains about 75 single-choice or multiple-choice questions, in English. The difficulty level is calibrated to assess in-depth knowledge of the framework, going well beyond everyday use. Questions often deal with edge cases, default behaviors, or rarely used configuration options that are essential for understanding how the framework works internally.

There are two levels of Symfony certification. The first validates a solid grasp of the framework's core concepts. The second, more demanding, is aimed at experienced developers who can design complex architectures and solve advanced problems. Choosing the right level depends on your experience and professional goals.

**Preparation resources:**

- The official Symfony documentation
- SymfonyCasts courses
- Hands-on experience on real projects
- The Sensiolabs University e-learning platform, which offers 20 daily practice questions that reproduce the certification format

### Twig Certified Designer

This certification validates frontend skills within Symfony projects. The "Twig Certified Designer" credential is aimed at developers and integrators who need to master the template engine.

Candidates must know Twig's main tags, functions, and filters, as documented in the official "Templates for Designers" section. The exam lasts about 30 minutes and can be prepared in a few days with focused, dedicated practice. The Twig certification is especially interesting for front-end profiles or integrators who regularly work on Symfony projects without necessarily mastering the whole framework. It validates a precise skill that is directly usable day to day. For candidates who want to anticipate the template engine's evolution, the article on [what to expect in Twig 4](/article/twig-4-ce-que-lon-pourrait-attendre) (in French) gives an overview of upcoming changes.

### Sylius Certified Developer

Ideal for e-commerce developers using Symfony, this certification demonstrates expertise with one of the most flexible open-source e-commerce frameworks on the market. [Sylius is the e-commerce solution of the Symfony framework](/article/sylius-la-solution-e-commerce-du-framework-symfony) (in French), which makes it a natural choice for teams already familiar with the framework and for [e-commerce projects](/secteur/e-commerce) (in French) that demand flexibility and performance. Our [Sylius e-commerce](/ecommerce-sylius) (in French) offering relies on certified developers to guarantee project quality.

The assessment covers:

- Sylius's modular architecture
- Product and order management
- Customer and payment management
- Extension mechanisms (events, hooks, service overrides)
- Testing best practices

The online exam lasts 90 minutes. Preparation relies on official resources, technical documentation, and customizing projects locally. Working on a real Sylius project remains the best way to prepare, because the exam assesses your ability to solve real problems tied to e-commerce development.

## Preparing for the Symfony Certification: A 6-Step Plan

Before you start, identify your strengths and gaps across the exam's 14 topics. If you work with Symfony daily, you probably master routing and controllers. But do you know compiler passes? Form data transformers?

1. **Assess your level**: list the 14 topics and rate your confidence on each
2. **Study the [official Symfony documentation](https://symfony.com/doc/current/index.html)**: do not limit yourself to getting-started guides, explore the reference pages and advanced options
3. **Practice with [SymfonyCasts](https://symfonycasts.com/)**: the video courses cover each topic and explain the "why" behind each mechanism
4. **Train with practice exams** on the [certification platform](https://certification.symfony.com/) to get familiar with the format
5. **Read the framework's source code**: this is the step that separates well-prepared candidates from those who pass with room to spare. Understanding the implementation of HttpKernel or the Security component transforms your understanding of the framework
6. **Schedule and take the exam**: 90 minutes, 75 questions, online through SymfonyConnect. Book a quiet time slot

### Advice From Certified Developers

Do not underestimate the Form component. It is the topic where candidates lose the most points. Data transformers, form events, and custom rendering call for specific preparation.

Master the service container in depth. Autowiring simplifies everyday use, but the exam tests what happens underneath: compiler passes, service locators, tagged services.

Read the questions slowly. The exam is in English and nuances matter. A question about the "default" behavior expects a different answer than a question about the "recommended" behavior.

## PHP and Laravel Certifications

### Zend Certified PHP Engineer / PHP Foundation

Although Zend no longer offers regularly updated sessions, this certification remains a recognized industry standard. It validates in-depth mastery of the PHP language, covering syntax, control structures, object-oriented programming, error handling, performance optimization, security, and database interactions.

The exam contains 70 questions to complete in 90 minutes. Several preparation platforms exist, including OpenClassrooms courses and Udemy courses. For Symfony developers, this certification is an excellent complement: it reinforces the language fundamentals that the whole framework rests on.

### Laravel Certification

The Laravel certification validates the framework's core skills: routing, Blade templating, Eloquent ORM, middleware, testing, queues, and broadcasting. The online exam lasts about one hour and covers the essential concepts of modern application development.

Preparation resources include Laravel Bootcamp, Laracasts tutorials, and the official documentation. Laravel and Symfony share many underlying components, which makes the transition between the two frameworks more natural for a certified developer.

## Docker Certification

### Docker Certified Associate (DCA)

Recognized worldwide, this certification validates containerization expertise: image management, volume configuration, Docker networking, security protocols, and Swarm orchestration.

The 90-minute exam contains 55 questions and is highly valued by fullstack developers, DevOps professionals, and software architects. Our article on [Docker in production](/article/pourquoi-docker-est-indispensable-en-production-aujourdhui) (in French) covers the key concepts to master for this certification. In a context where containerization has become a deployment standard, this certification adds credibility for technical profiles who want to show versatility beyond pure application development.

## JavaScript Certifications: React, Vue, Node.js

### React Certification (Coursera/Meta)

Hosted on Coursera and backed by Meta, this certification validates React skills: functional components, state management with Hooks, React Router implementation, API integration, testing, and performance optimization.

The exam combines quizzes and independent project implementation. Several months of hands-on React experience are recommended to pass. This certification is particularly relevant for fullstack developers who combine a Symfony backend with a React frontend.

### Vue.js Developer Certification (Vue School)

Vue School offers a recognized certification covering the essentials of [Vue 3](/developpement-vuejs) (in French): core syntax, Composition API, directives, props, event handling, state management solutions (Vuex or Pinia), Vue Router implementation, and industry best practices.

The online exam lasts about one hour, with preparation available through interactive modules. For fullstack developers who combine Vue.js with a PHP backend, our [comparison of JavaScript frameworks](/article/quel-framework-javascript-choisir-node-js-react-js-vue-js-ou-angular) (in French) helps you choose the right front-end technology.

### OpenJS Node.js Application

Certified by the OpenJS Foundation, this certification validates backend application development with Node.js: module management, asynchronous programming with promises and async/await patterns, REST API creation, error handling, testing methodologies, and application security.
